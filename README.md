# Character Spawn

A Dalamud plugin for FINAL FANTASY XIV that lets you spawn, position, animate, and stage custom characters, NPCs, and monsters locally on your screen.

## Features
- **Two-Step Workflow**:
  - **Step 1 (Character Library)**: Create and save character templates (Glamourer/Penumbra designs, MCDF files, monsters/NPCs, or player clones).
  - **Step 2 (Stage & Scene)**: Place characters on the map, adjust positions via 3D gizmos & UI controls, configure animations, expressions, head tracking, and nameplates.
- **Stagehand-like Presets**:
  - Save multi-actor scenes tied to specific zones (`TerritoryType`).
  - One-click Show / Hide.
  - Auto-spawn characters upon entering configured zones.
- **3D Gizmo & Precision Controls**:
  - Drag XYZ manipulators & Yaw rotation rings in 3D screen space.
  - Fine-tune adjustments (+0.1, -0.1), snap to player position, and snap to floor.
- **Animation & Expression Staging**:
  - Full `ActionTimeline` animation search and playback.
  - Seamless loop option for one-shot emotes.
  - Independent facial expression control.
  - Look-at player (head tracking) toggle.
- **Nameplates & Targetability**:
  - Custom display names or hide nameplates completely.
  - Targetable or unclickable background actor toggle.
  - No collision / full walkthrough.
